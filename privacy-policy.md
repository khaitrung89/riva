# RIVA Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026

App package: `com.ourtour24h.app`

RIVA — Moments in Motion. RIVA helps users manage trip information, split travel expenses, maintain a travel profile, and share travel video or live moments. This policy describes the data practices intended for the Android app release.

## Data We May Collect

RIVA may collect or process the following information when users choose to use the related features:

- Account information, such as Google/Firebase sign-in identity, email address, profile name, avatar, and basic profile details.
- Trip information, such as trip names, members, expenses, transfers, settlement data, and local sync state.
- Profile and social activity, such as profile details, video posts, view counts, reactions, comments, shares, and related sync records.
- Media information, such as videos, thumbnails, file metadata, upload status, and Google Drive file references when users upload travel videos.
- Livestream-related information, such as stream setup data when users use live features.
- Device and app diagnostics, such as crash, sync, and operational state that may be generated by Android, Firebase, Google Play, or related SDKs.

## Why We Use Data

We use data to:

- Provide trip planning and expense-splitting features.
- Keep trip, profile, video, and engagement records available locally and synced in the background.
- Authenticate users and protect account-scoped data.
- Upload, publish, display, and moderate user-selected travel videos.
- Support livestream and media features when users choose to use them.
- Diagnose bugs, improve app reliability, and respond to support requests.

## Permissions

RIVA may request these Android permissions:

- Internet access, to sign in and sync data with remote services.
- Camera access, to support live/video features.
- Microphone access, to support live/video features.

Users can deny or revoke camera and microphone permissions in Android settings. Features that need those permissions may not work without them.

## Third-Party Services

RIVA may use Google and Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, Google Drive authorization/upload features, Google Play services, and Google Play distribution tools. These services may process data according to their own terms and privacy policies.

## Data Storage And Sync

The app is designed to be offline-first. Some user data is stored locally on the device and can be synced to remote services in the background. Local data may remain on the device until the user clears app data, uninstalls the app, or uses future deletion features provided by the app.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell user data. User content may be shared with the services needed to operate app features, such as Google/Firebase services for authentication, storage, sync, media upload, and app diagnostics. User-created public or shared content may be visible to other app users according to the app feature being used.

## Data Deletion

Users can request help with account or data deletion through the support channel listed below. Some local data can also be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app from the Android device. Remote data deletion may require account verification so that the correct user records can be identified.

## Children

RIVA is not designed as a children-directed app. If future release settings target younger audiences, the Play Console listing and this policy must be reviewed and updated before publication.
