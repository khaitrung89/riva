# RIVA Child Safety Standards

Effective date: August 18, 2026

App name: RIVA

App package: `com.ourtour24h.app`

RIVA is intended for users who are 18 years old or older. RIVA is not designed for children, and children are not part of the intended target audience.

## Prohibited Content And Conduct

RIVA prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexualization of minors, sextortion, trafficking of minors, predatory behavior toward minors, and any attempt to create, upload, share, request, promote, or distribute content that exploits or endangers children.

RIVA also prohibits user-generated content or behavior that includes harassment, threats, sexually explicit material involving minors, illegal activity, or any content that violates Google Play Developer Program Policies or applicable child safety laws.

## User Reports And Safety Feedback

Users can report safety concerns, objectionable content, suspected CSAE/CSAM, abuse, account issues, or policy violations by opening the in-app "Safety & support" link or by using the public support channel:

https://github.com/khaitrung89/riva/issues

Users should include the app version, device model, account email used for sign-in, a description of the concern, and any non-sensitive evidence that helps identify the content or account. Users must not submit passwords, private documents, payment card details, or illegal content in a public report.

Safety reports may include reasons such as child safety, sexual content, grooming, exploitation, violence, self-harm, harassment, scam, spam, or other abuse. Reports involving child safety, suspected grooming, exploitation, CSAE, or CSAM are treated as highest priority.

## Review And Enforcement

When RIVA receives an abuse or safety report, the developer will review the report and take appropriate action. Actions may include removing or hiding content, restricting accounts, preserving evidence where legally required, and cooperating with platform or legal processes.

If RIVA obtains actual knowledge of confirmed CSAM or CSAE content, the developer will take appropriate action, including removing the content where technically possible and reporting confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or another relevant regional authority when required by applicable law.

RIVA may use upload validation, thumbnail generation, media review, moderation records, and manual review workflows to help identify unsafe user-generated content. Automated checks are support tools and do not replace user reports, developer review, enforcement, or legally required escalation.

## Child Safety Contact

Google Play or users can contact the developer about child safety issues through:

https://github.com/khaitrung89/riva/issues

This contact is monitored for app release, privacy, abuse, and child safety reports. A dedicated safety email or website may replace this channel before public production distribution.

## Age Access

RIVA requires users to confirm that they are 18 years old or older before signing in. If release settings, features, or store listing assets are changed to target younger users in the future, this policy, the privacy policy, app content declarations, and app safety controls must be reviewed before publication.
